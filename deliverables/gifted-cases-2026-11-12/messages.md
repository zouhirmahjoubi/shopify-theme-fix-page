# Gifted cases: messages

Copy, change the name, send. Everything here is written to be sent by you, in your own name.

## The four rules

1. Send to people with no tie to you or the shop. Not family, not anyone who works with you.
2. Ask for an honest review. Never ask for a good one, and never ask for a star count.
3. Every review that comes in stays up, good or bad.
4. Every gifted review says the case was a gift: one line from the reviewer, and your reply under it.

## 1. Invite

> Hi [name], I run Casify, a small shop that prints phone cases. I'd like to send you one, free.
>
> One ask: after a few days with it, write an honest review on our site. Any rating, your own words. Please mention that the case was a gift.
>
> You don't have to post anything anywhere else. Want one?

## 2. When they say yes

> Great. I need four things:
>
> 1. The case you want (send me the link from casify.shop)
> 2. Your phone model
> 3. Your name and US shipping address
> 4. An email address for the order and the tracking
>
> Each case is printed to order. Most arrive within 3 weeks, and you get a tracking email when it ships.

## 3. When the case has arrived (optional nudge)

> Hi [name], your case should be with you by now. A review email from Casify is on its way. Be honest: any rating, your own words, a photo if you like. Please add one line saying Casify sent you the case as a gift. Thank you.

## 4. Your reply under each gifted review

Post this in Judge.me as a public reply, the same day the review comes in:

> Casify sent this case as a gift. The rating and the words are the reviewer's own.

Where: Shopify admin > Apps > Judge.me > Reviews > Reply (to the right of the review) > Publicly in widgets > paste > Send reply.

Keep the first sentence word for word. This reply is the switch: the store's review sections read it and show "Gifted case" in place of "Verified buyer". There is no other setting to turn on, in the theme or in Judge.me. Live since 2026-11-14.

It only works on a review from someone who got their case through a $0 order in Shopify, because Judge.me has to mark the review verified first. Imported reviews never show, with or without the reply.

## If someone asks what to write

> Whatever is true for you. How it fits, how the print looks, anything you don't like. Short is fine.
