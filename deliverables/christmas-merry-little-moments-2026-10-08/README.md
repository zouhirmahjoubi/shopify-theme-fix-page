# Merry Little Moments

One Christmas phone-case design in midnight navy, ivory lettering, snowy evergreen foliage, cranberry berries, and gold stars. Exact text: MERRY little MOMENTS.

The original artwork and wider bleed master were made with built-in ImageGen. The native bleed master is 991 × 1587 pixels; the original artwork is 887 × 1774. Template exports are resampled to each measured canvas and tagged 300 DPI; that metadata does not add native detail.

Research: Etsy's Spring/Summer 2026 seller report identifies demand for playful accessories and polka-dot phone cases. Snowfall dots are a seasonal design interpretation, not a sales prediction. Printify's 2026 holiday guide recommends cohesive themes such as cozy cabin and retro Christmas.

- https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1473931456647
- https://printify.com/blog/holiday-guide/

Production checks: Background reaches all four sides of each exported print canvas; no guide lines in print files; lettering stays within the measured front face and outside the camera exclusion box. Samsung S26 models and two iPhone models use lower lettering placement because their camera exclusion areas reach farther down.

The 28 template exports and overlays use existing local Casestry templates. These are reference checks.
