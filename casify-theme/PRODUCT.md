## Platform

web

## Users

Shoppers buying a case for one specific phone, mostly iPhone (some Samsung), arriving from ads, search, collections and the home page, largely on mobile. Their job: confirm the case fits their exact model, like how it looks, know what it costs delivered, and check out without doubt. (Inferred from the catalog and store copy; not yet confirmed by the merchant.)

## Product Purpose

Casify is a phone case brand selling iPhone and Samsung cases (confirmed by the merchant, 2026-09-15), through a Shopify store: about 10 active products at $23.99 to $49.99, vendor Casify, each with 44 to 81 variants built from a style or colour option and a phone-model option. The product page exists to turn a visitor into an add-to-cart with the right model selected, and to lift order value through multi-case packs. Success is add-to-cart and checkout completion with no model mistakes and no price surprises.

## Positioning

An online shop dressed as an old-fashioned candy shop ("Est. 1920"): striped awnings, hanging shop signs, peppermints, sweets on the counter. The presentation is the brand's distinctive frame. Product performance is not a store-wide claim; each case's facts belong to that case. (Positioning inferred from the theme; merchant to confirm wording.)

## Operating Context

- Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme: Liquid sections, JSON templates (`templates/product.json` is the default for every product and uses "The Order Slip"; `product.convert.json` uses "The Shop Counter" and can be assigned per product), section groups for header and footer.
- Self-hosted GSAP 3.15 with all plugins loads on every page (`snippets/gsap.liquid`).
- The storefront is password-protected during the build; themes ship as dated unpublished themes via Shopify CLI for the merchant to preview and publish.
- Static preview harnesses live in `dev/` (ignored by `.shopifyignore`).

- Typical options: option 1 is a style or colour (for example "Magnetic Case Camera Cutout Transparent"), option 2 is the phone model, often 20 to 40 values.
- Product descriptions are currently empty and product images carry no alt text; some products have around 50 images.
- Multi-case packs (2 and 3 cases) must be backed by real Shopify automatic volume discounts so the page price equals the checkout price. Nothing is created or changed in the store without the merchant's explicit confirmation.
