## Blocking

1. Turning Reduce Motion on with the page open scrolls the page to the top.
   Reproduced at 768 and 375 (`harness/u1.2/verify-exp4/jump.json`): carousel scrolled into view (scrollY 2810 at
   768, 2703 at 375), `emulate_media(reduced_motion='reduce')`; at 100 ms scrollY unchanged, by 1.6 s one scroll
   event to 0 and the carousel sits 2810 px below the viewport; it stays at 0 after switching back. The u1.2-fix
   probe shows the same (`harness/u1.2/fix/probe.json` step 2: `sy` 2810 then 0), its step-4 screenshot timeout is
   this jump, not the "first load below the fold" its note gives. ScrollTrigger count goes 8 to 0, i.e. all
   triggers on the page are the carousel's. Isolation (`jump2.json`, 768): A carousel live 2810 -> 0; B same page
   after `root._cvcrDestroy()` 2810 -> 2810 (no jump); C scrolled 600 px past the carousel 3410 -> 0. The jump comes
   from the carousel's matchMedia cleanup, not the harness or another unit.
   Shopper effect: a shopper who switches on Reduce Motion (often for motion discomfort) while choosing a case
   loses their place and is thrown to the top of the product page. This is the live-switch path PLAN.md:19 now
   relies on, introduced by the fix.
   Fix direction (U1.2 JS only): in the matchMedia cleanup, keep the position across the revert, for example read
   `window.scrollY` before `stopRide()` / the context revert and restore it after (`ScrollTrigger.refresh()` then
   `window.scrollTo(0, y)`), or find which reverted piece (SplitText mask, ride canvas removal, killed triggers)
   resets scroll and revert it without the reset. Re-prove with `jump.py`.
