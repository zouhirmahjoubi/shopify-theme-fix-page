## Motion (PLAN motion 1 to 6)

- Motion CDN and three.js imports are gone (grep finds no jsdelivr, unpkg or `import()`). GSAP only. All motion
  runs inside `gsap.matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)')`, and the sticky ScrollTrigger runs in
  `gsap.context`.
- Add to bag: the form keeps `data-cart-add-form`. best-product.js only gates on its own submit listener
  (`preventDefault` when the model is missing). cart.js then posts `/cart/add.js` and emits `cart:added` with `from`
  (the main or sticky button) and `img`, so cv-commerce-motion.js flies the case to the bag. The main photo and the
  sticky photo carry `data-cvm-fly-img`, and the sticky photo follows variant changes. The page stays. The drawer
  opens only when `cv_open_drawer_on_add` is on.
- Buy now: press squash. The busy, done and error states come from `data-cvm-btn`. The pack highlight and total
  roll come from INF-3 attributes.
- Photo crossfade on variant change, a hop on the chosen pill, Flip on "Show all models" / "Show fewer models",
  story cards on `ScrollTrigger.batch`, fit callouts in sequence, and a short ring pulse on the magnetic card in
  place of the three.js glow. A sticky Add bar appears on ScrollTrigger when the main buttons leave the viewport.
- Theme editor: `shopify:section:load` / `unload` / `select` / `block:select` are handled. Listeners sit on an
  AbortController and init can run more than once safely.
- No `data-cv-reveal` on prices, pickers, buttons or the buy column. The no-JS state is readable.
