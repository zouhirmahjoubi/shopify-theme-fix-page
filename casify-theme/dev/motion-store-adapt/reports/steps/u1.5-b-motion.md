## Changes
- assets/cv-pages.js
  - `motion` is live from `gsap.matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)')` (no static matchMedia read).
  - Collection modules are per-root `initSign(el)`, `initPackMeter(el)`, `initAisle(el)`, each returning a stop
    function; `boot(document)` on load, `boot(e.target)` on `shopify:section:load`, `teardown(e.target)` on
    `shopify:section:unload`. Document/window listeners go through `listen()` and are removed on stop
    (pack meter: cv:cart, cart:drawer:refreshed, cart:refresh, cart:added, pageshow; aisle: popstate). Signs run
    inside their own gsap.matchMedia (ScrollTriggers and the lean quickTo revert with it). Aisle stop aborts the
    in-flight fetch and ignores late responses.
  - packMeter: moves `data-cvm-bag` to the next empty box (the row once all three are full). On `cart:added`
    from `quick-pick` or `card` with the meter in view, the box update is held 1000 ms so the lid opens as the
    commerce token lands; quick pick no longer triggers a redundant /cart.js fetch (its cv:cart carries the cart).
  - ruleBook / contact unchanged apart from the live `motion` flag for smooth scrolling.
- assets/cv-collection-motion.js (new): ScrollTrigger.batch card drop (`.cvsh-card` inside `.cvl__cell`,
  y -24, rotation +-1.5, opacity only, back.out(1.4) 0.5 s, stagger 0.06, start top 92%, once) inside
  gsap.matchMedia; results swaps (MutationObserver on `[data-cvl-swap="results"]`) stock new cells below the fold
  only (in-view cells keep the cv-pages.js Flip onEnter); focusin shows a waiting card at once. Single-variant Add:
  window capture/bubble submit listeners around cart.js keep the candy-sign markup, put cart.js's busy text into
  `[data-cvm-label]`, toggle is-busy, then is-done ("Added to bag" from the drawer's data-i18n-added) when
  `cart:added {source:'card'}` came for that button, else is-error; back to idle after 1.6 s / 1.2 s.
  Section load/unload handled per `[data-cvl]` root.
- snippets/cv-pack-meter.liquid: `data-cvm-bag` on the first empty box (on the `ul` when three are filled).
- snippets/cv-showcase-card.liquid: add-mode button gets `data-cvm-btn`, its label span `data-cvm-label`;
  the media figure gets `data-cvm-fly-img` (INF-2 item 5, second half).
