## Defect fixed: pack pills pushed 90 px right

- Seen in default 375/768/1440: the 1 case / 2 cases / 3 cases pills started about 90 px in from the
  column edge while every other control sat on it.
- Cause (probe_look.py): the travelling outline span .cvcr__packs-hl (data-cvm-pack-highlight) was
  display block but position static, so it took a flex slot (82 px plus the 8 px gap). First pill left
  was 106 at 375 and 768.
- Fix, assets/cv-pdp-carousel.css: .cvcr__packs gets position relative; new rule
  `.cvcr__packs[data-cvm-packs] > .cvcr__packs-hl { position: absolute; left: 0; top: 0; z-index: 3;
  pointer-events: none; }` (z-index 3 so the ring draws over the opaque pills; display still comes from
  cv-commerce-motion.css, so no ring without the motion module).
- Re-probe: first pill left 16 (column edge) at 375 and 768; the outline sits over the checked pill
  (left 97, width 82 = pill 2). Screenshots stage-375.png / stage-768.png in the step scratch folder.
- Also replaced the em dash in the file's line 1 developer comment (hook gate). dash-scan: PASS.

## Defect fixed: colour cards missing on the 3D ride

- default-768.png (shoot.py) and probe_stage.py at 768x1024 and 375x812 (page loaded at top, pack row
  scrolled into view, 4.5 s wait): canopy, column and grown poles, but no colour cards, lights off, and
  no drag hint or pause button (those come from onReady at the end of the intro).
- Cause: the frame rate in headless Chromium is 3 fps (probe, software WebGL). playIntro's 2.6 s
  timeline was frozen near 0.8 s (poles done, cards at pop 0); GSAP lag smoothing stretches the intro
  on slow frames, and the cards are the colour photos, so the ride stayed empty. Same class of defect
  as u1.2-a (content behind an entrance).
- Fix, assets/cv-pdp-carousel.js playIntro: a 4 s wall-clock guard,
  `setTimeout(() => { if (!destroyed && !introDone) tl.progress(1); }, 4000)`, cleared through the
  stage's cleanups on destroy. node --check passes.
- Re-probe at 768 (stage-768.png in the step scratch folder): cards (Russet Brown front, Black, Blue),
  lights on and "Pause the ride" shown, pack pills on the column edge.
