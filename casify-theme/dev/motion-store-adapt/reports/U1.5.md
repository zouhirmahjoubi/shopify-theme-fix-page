## Motion (PLAN motion 1 to 6)

- Lifecycle: `cv-pages.js` collection modules are per root (`initSign`, `initPackMeter`, `initAisle`), booted on
  load and `shopify:section:load`, torn down on `shopify:section:unload`. Document and window listeners go through
  `listen()` and are removed on stop; the aisle aborts its in-flight fetch. `motion` is live from `gsap.matchMedia`.
- Shelf-stocking card drop (`cv-collection-motion.js`): `ScrollTrigger.batch` on `.cvsh-card`, y -24, rotation
  +-1.5, back.out(1.4) 0.5 s, stagger 0.06, once. Result swaps stock new below-fold cells only; focusin shows a
  waiting card at once.
- Single-variant Add: card form keeps `data-cart-add-form`; button has `data-cvm-btn`, label span
  `data-cvm-label`. Busy text goes into the label, `is-busy` then `is-done` ("Added to bag") on
  `cart:added {source:'card'}`, else `is-error`. This works around cart.js wiping the sign markup
  (`requests/U1.5.md` item 3).
- Fly into the pack meter: `data-cvm-bag` sits on the next empty box (the row when three are full). On
  `cart:added` from `quick-pick` or `card` with the meter in view, the box update waits 1000 ms so the lid opens as
  the token lands.
- Filter feedback: badge pop (scale 0.3 to 1, back.out(2)); result count roll with `aria-busy` while rolling so
  only the final number is announced; Flip on chips that stay, pop for new chips; results Flip now matches cards
  (server cells get `data-flip-id`). A swap still moving is finished before the next one.
- Pack line stamp when the bag count crosses 2 or 3 (scale 1.18, rotation -6 to 0, back.out(2)).
- Reduced motion: no drop, no fly, final count at once, no transforms (probed).
