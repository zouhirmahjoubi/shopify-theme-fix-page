  typed quantity and Remove in the capture phase with `preventDefault` (cart.js skips on

  `?section_id`), swaps lines and summary, dispatches `cart:refresh`. Rapid taps on one line merge into one

  through `data-cvm-stamp` (cv-commerce-motion.js).

  (never delays submit). Empty state bag swings once.

  No Flip is used (not loaded on /cart).
