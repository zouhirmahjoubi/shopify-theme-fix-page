   GSAP write and the 4px hop is damped. Fix as the Buy now squash does (`transition:'none'` in the from vars), or drop
   `transform` from that transition.

   the bar height while `.is-shown`.

   nothing (`best-gallery__arrow`, section:161 and :181). Hiding the arrows with `.best-js-only` would remove two dead
