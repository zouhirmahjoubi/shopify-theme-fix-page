Questions at the counter

How long does shipping take?

Orders leave us within 1 to 3 business days, and shipping is free.

Can I send it back?

Yes. You have 30 days to return it.

How do pack prices work?

With 2 cases in your bag each one is 18% off, and with 3 or more each one is 31% off. The saving comes off automatically at checkout, so the total on your button is what the cases cost, before any tax.

What does Buy now do?

It puts the case and pack you chose in your bag and takes you straight to checkout. Add to bag keeps you shopping instead.

How do I check my phone model?

On an iPhone, open Settings, then General, then About, and look for Model Name. On a Samsung, open Settings, then About phone.

Will it fit my phone?

Pick your phone in the Model list. It only shows the models this case is made for.
