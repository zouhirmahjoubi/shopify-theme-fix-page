
## From u1.1-fix (2026-10-26): no-JS pack quantity on the Order Slip

sections/cv-product.liquid:156 renders the native-post quantity as `value="{{ active_qty }}"`. Without
JavaScript the pack buttons and the per-case selects (cv-pack-slots.liquid, no `name`) do nothing, so a
no-JS shopper on a page whose default pack is 2 or 3 posts 2 or 3 of one variant and cannot pick 1.
U1.1 fixed the same defect on the Shop Counter: quantity renders `value="1"` (cv-product.js writes the
pack quantity when it runs), and the packs, savings meter, `.cvpk` slots, sticky bar and pack total
hide under `html.no-js`, with a `<noscript>` one-case total in the button. Suggest the same for the slip
(its own CSS file; the counter rule is scoped to `.cvc .cvpk`). The shared snippet already leaves the
phone model out of the Case 1 summary while the model placeholder is on.
