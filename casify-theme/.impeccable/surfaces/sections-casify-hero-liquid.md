## Scope

Homepage hero · Persuade

## Audience and job

Mobile-first phone-case shoppers identify their iPhone 18 Pro or Pro Max, choose an available colour, and follow the exact variant shopping link.

## Proof and constraints

Use only live product variants and confirmed service facts. The supplied storyboard controls composition and finite timing, but its generic phones and proposed colours are not product evidence. Without validated GLBs, real product photos remain the visible fallback and no synthetic 3D hardware ships.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make exact-model choice the hero's main event inside Casify's established candy shop, not a detached tech campaign.

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper, cocoa ink, candy red, muted teal, aged gold, striped awning, photo tickets, typewriter controls, and physically caused depth.

STORY: See both supported sizes, choose the exact model and available colour, then shop the matching live variant.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 1440x900, a 44% left copy-and-commerce column faces a 56% right studio window holding two complete real-product photo tickets; model, colour, CTA, variant state, and motion controls remain visible.

FORM: Direct established-world extension; seed established-system-candy-matinee. Signature interaction: one interruptible 4.8-second GSAP sequence moves from pair to finish, detail, two sizes, colour ribbon, and a calm stop; mobile defaults static and reduced motion is immediate.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Unresolved decisions

Validated Pro and Pro Max GLBs remain an optional future enhancement; real Shopify product images are the production experience for this pass.
