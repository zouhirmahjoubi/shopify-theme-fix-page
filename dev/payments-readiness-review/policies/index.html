Shipping Policy Updated preview draft

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how Casify processes and ships orders.

Order Processing

Orders typically leave our fulfillment location in the United States within 1–2 business days of purchase, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. You'll receive a confirmation email once your order ships, including tracking information where available.

Free Shipping

Shipping is free on every order, with no minimum purchase required. Products fulfilled through Printify have an estimated delivery time of 4–6 business days after shipment. Other products have an estimated delivery time of 8–12 business days after shipment. Estimated delivery times for your order are shown at checkout, based on your delivery address and the products in your cart. These are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. The 1–2 business day processing period is separate from the delivery estimates.

International Shipping & Customs

We ship to a number of international destinations. International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes, and customs fees charged once the package reaches its destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are outside our control.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with tracking information so you can follow its progress.

Lost or Delayed Packages

If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated delivery window, contact us at zouhir.mahjoubi@zmdesign.me and we'll look into it with the carrier on your behalf.

Contact Us

Questions about shipping? Email zouhir.mahjoubi@zmdesign.me or call +212 653 926 665.

Return Policy Current published terms; confirmation pending

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Thank you for shopping with Casify. We want you to be completely happy with your purchase. This policy explains how returns, exchanges, and refunds work.

30-Day Return Window

You may return an eligible item within 30 days of delivery for any reason, including a simple change of mind. To start a return, email us at zouhir.mahjoubi@zmdesign.me with your order number. We'll send you a prepaid return shipping label.

Condition of Returned Items

Items should be returned in the condition you received them, with any included accessories. Normal signs that you tried the item on or tested it are fine — you do not need to keep the original packaging.

Refunds

Once your return is scanned by the carrier, we process a full refund to your original payment method. Refunds are typically issued within 5–10 business days of the carrier scan, though your bank or card issuer may take additional time to post the funds.

Exchanges

Need a different model or finish? Contact us and we'll arrange an exchange instead of a refund wherever possible.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery with photos of the item and packaging, and we'll arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

Non-Returnable Items

Final sale and clearance items, and gift cards, are not eligible for return unless defective.

Contact Us

Questions about a return or refund? Email zouhir.mahjoubi@zmdesign.me or call +212 653 926 665. You can also reach us at centre sidi ghanem essaouira, 44000 essaouira, Morocco.
