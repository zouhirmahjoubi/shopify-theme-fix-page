US application checklist

  1. Confirm the US entity type, IRS legal name and EIN, and actual physical US business address where operations primarily occur. Company registration and a bank account alone do not settle the country and operating-address review.
  2. For an eligible US registered company, use the representative’s true residential address. A non-US resident without an SSN/ITIN may use the offered passport path. Individual and sole-proprietor accounts require US residence and a valid SSN/ITIN.
  3. Confirm the payout bank account is a full US checking account in USD and supports ACH. Enable two-step authentication.
  4. The store owner completes activation and verification in Shopify’s secure admin. Personal and business details must match valid original documents exactly.
  5. After final review, add approved refund, shipping, terms, and contact text to Shopify’s checkout Policies. The connected store currently has only its privacy policy configured there.
  6. Use truthful product descriptions, authentic reviews and claims, and delivery promises the business can meet. Store fixes cannot guarantee Payments approval.

Official references: US verification requirements, Account setup, Customer-facing disclosures, Business risk review.
