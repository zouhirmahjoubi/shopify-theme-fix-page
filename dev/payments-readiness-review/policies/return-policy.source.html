Thank you for shopping with Casify. We want you to be completely happy with your purchase. This policy explains how returns, exchanges, and refunds work.

30-Day Return Window

You may return an eligible item within 30 days of delivery for any reason, including a simple change of mind. To start a return, email us at zouhir.mahjoubi@zmdesign.me with your order number. We'll send you a prepaid return shipping label.

Condition of Returned Items

Items should be returned in the condition you received them, with any included accessories. Normal signs that you tried the item on or tested it are fine — you do not need to keep the original packaging.

Refunds

Once your return is scanned by the carrier, we process a full refund to your original payment method. Refunds are typically issued within 5–10 business days of the carrier scan, though your bank or card issuer may take additional time to post the funds.

Exchanges

Need a different model or finish? Contact us and we'll arrange an exchange instead of a refund wherever possible.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery with photos of the item and packaging, and we'll arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

Non-Returnable Items

Final sale and clearance items, and gift cards, are not eligible for return unless defective.
