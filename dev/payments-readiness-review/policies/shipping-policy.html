This policy explains how Casify processes and ships orders.

Order Processing

Orders typically leave our fulfillment location in the United States within 1–2 business days of purchase, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. You'll receive a confirmation email once your order ships, including tracking information where available.

Free Shipping

Shipping is free on every order, with no minimum purchase required. Products fulfilled through Printify have an estimated delivery time of 4–6 business days after shipment. Other products have an estimated delivery time of 8–12 business days after shipment. Estimated delivery times for your order are shown at checkout, based on your delivery address and the products in your cart. These are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. The 1–2 business day processing period is separate from the delivery estimates.

International Shipping & Customs

We ship to a number of international destinations. International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes, and customs fees charged once the package reaches its destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are outside our control.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with tracking information so you can follow its progress.

Lost or Delayed Packages

If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated delivery window, contact us at zouhir.mahjoubi@zmdesign.me and we'll look into it with the carrier on your behalf.
