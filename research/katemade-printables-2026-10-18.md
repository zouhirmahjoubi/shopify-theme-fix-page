## How the business works

1. **Low-cost digital products.** Each design is made once and sold any
   number of times. Small items sell for $3–5, play sets for $6–8, and there
   are no shipping or stock costs.
2. **The price always looks discounted.** The list price is roughly doubled
   and the item is shown at 40–50% off.
3. **Titles are packed with keywords.** One title covers the product (paper
   flower vase), the activity (colour your own), the occasions (Teacher
   Appreciation, Mother's Day) and the format (DIY, PDF).
4. **One design is sold several ways.** The same craft comes as a colouring
   version and a full-colour version, with tags in English and German.
5. **Video drives sales.** YouTube tutorials show the finished build and link
   to the shop. Pinterest pins say "make the same paper craft at home using
   templates designed by me".
6. **New items come out every few days.** There were new listings on
   09-28, 10-01 and 10-17. Seasonal themes (Mother's Day, teacher gifts,
   holidays) bring in search traffic.
7. **The buyers are parents and teachers.** The crafts are simple: print,
   colour, cut, glue. The listings say when an adult should help.

## How this could apply to us

- The Shopify connector here has digital-product tools
  (`create-digital-product`, `upload-digital-product-file`,
  `publish-digital-product`). A printable PDF could be sold from the store
  with no fulfilment.
- `deliverables/` already has printable work (school schedule posters, the
  Christmas set), so making designs is not a new skill.
- Designs must be original. Use this shop to learn the business model and
  the product format. Do not copy its artwork, its templates or its listing
  text.
